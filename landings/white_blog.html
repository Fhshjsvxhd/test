10 Tips for a Healthier Morning Routine

Published on the blog · 7 min read

Starting the day with intention is one of the simplest ways to feel better all day long. In this article we share ten small habits that add up to a big difference over time.

1. Drink a glass of water first thing

After eight hours of sleep your body is mildly dehydrated. A full glass of room-temperature water helps kickstart digestion and wake up the nervous system.

2. Stretch for five minutes

Gentle stretches loosen up stiff joints and signal the body that the day has begun. Focus on the neck, shoulders and hips.

3. Eat a balanced breakfast

Aim for a mix of protein, complex carbs and fruit. Overnight oats with berries and almonds is a popular choice in our community.

4. Get natural light

Even five minutes of morning sun near a window helps regulate your circadian rhythm and improves sleep the following night.

5. Plan three priorities

Before opening email, jot down the three things that would make today a win. You will feel more focused and less reactive.
